used locally to verify the port, but must not be treated as cleared for public
redistribution until their original sources and licenses are resolved or the
files are replaced. Hashes and embedded attribution evidence are recorded in
`docs/porting/audio-provenance.md`.

The same provenance rule applies to any other bundled legacy asset whose
original license is not explicitly recorded. A GPL source license alone is not
evidence that every texture, sound or other asset is GPL-licensed.
